## 2026-05-22: Codex Instruction Capture

- Rule: Before starting a new Phase or subtask, maintain the corresponding `docs/phase-*.md` with task intent, scope, requirements, tests, acceptance criteria, and status.
- Rule: Save repeatable or important prompts in `docs/codex-prompts.md`; skip one-off bug-fix prompts unless they establish a durable rule.
- Rule: After each subtask, record completion details, changed files, tests, failures, and next steps in the phase document.
